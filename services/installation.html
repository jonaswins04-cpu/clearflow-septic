Septic System Installation

From the first bucket of dirt to the final commissioning check, we build the system that was designed — with our own equipment, so your job isn't waiting on someone else's schedule.

Call 778-392-6313 Request a Site Visit

What's included

A full installation covers everything between an approved design and a working, certified system:

  • Excavation and site prep, including access for equipment on tight or sloped lots
  • Setting tanks (septic and pump tanks) to grade and level
  • Running and pressure-testing force mains and distribution piping
  • Installing pumps, floats, and control panels, and wiring them to spec
  • Building distribution beds or engineered mounds to design
  • Backfill, rough grade, and final grade
  • Commissioning — confirming dose volumes, alarms, and pump cycling actually match the design before we call it done

Why we run our own equipment

A lot of septic contractors design the system, then subcontract the digging to whoever's available. That's where timelines slip and where what gets built starts to drift from what was drawn. We own our excavation equipment, so the crew on your site is the same outfit that did the design — no subcontractor roulette, no waiting on someone else's iron to free up.

What we document

Every installation wraps up with a certification package: as-built drawings, a commissioning field report, an installer declaration, and an owner's information sheet covering how the system works and what maintenance it needs. Within 30 days of completion, this supports the Letter of Certification (LoC) the Authorized Person files with your health authority.

What to expect for timeline

Straightforward conventional systems on accessible lots move faster than engineered mounds or sites with difficult access. Weather and ground conditions (frozen ground, spring runoff) also factor in. We'll give you a realistic window at the site visit — not an optimistic one that slips.
