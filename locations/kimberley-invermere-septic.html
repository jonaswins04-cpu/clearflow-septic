Septic Systems — Invermere & Columbia Valley

From Invermere and Radium north to Golden, the Columbia Valley is one of our two home-base regions — and its lakes and wetlands bring site conditions you don't see as often further south.

Call 778-392-6313 Request a Site Visit

A different set of site conditions

Properties closer to the lake and along the valley bottom can run into a higher water table than lots further up the bench — but that's not something we assume from an address, it's something we check at the site visit. Recreational and seasonal-use properties are also common through this valley, which can affect design flow considerations. Every recommendation comes from what the test pits and perc testing actually show on your specific lot, not from what part of the valley it's in.

What that means for a build here

  • Properties near water or in low-lying spots can run into water table challenges — confirmed by testing, not assumed beforehand
  • Seasonal-use properties (cabins, recreational lots) sometimes have different design flow considerations than a full-time residence — worth flagging when you call
  • Setback requirements from the lake, wetlands, and neighbouring wells factor into site layout here — we check those early so there are no surprises

Golden & Radium

Golden sits at the northern edge of our regular service area, and Radium and the hot springs corridor share similar valley geography with Invermere. As with everywhere else we work, what a specific property needs comes down to what we find on site — not a general assumption about the area.

Real estate work in the valley

A meaningful share of Columbia Valley properties are recreational or investment purchases, which means septic assessments tied to a sale come up often here — buyers wanting to know what they're inheriting on a lake property before subject removal.
